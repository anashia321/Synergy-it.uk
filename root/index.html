06 / FAQ

Questions businesses ask before they choose an IT partner.

What is managed IT support?

Managed IT support is an ongoing service model that keeps your systems, workflows and infrastructure reliable while helping teams solve problems before they escalate.

Do you support businesses across the UK?

Yes. We support businesses across the UK with remote, hybrid and office-based setups, including remote support for growing teams.

Is cyber security necessary for a growing business?

Yes. Cyber security is a foundational business risk issue, and practical controls matter just as much as technology itself.

How do I know if we need a technology review?

If your team is dealing with recurring issues, unclear responsibilities or slow growth caused by technology friction, a review can help clarify the next step.
