# Synergy IT SEO go-live checklist

This checklist covers the external steps that cannot be completed from the static site repository.

## Google Business Profile

Use the exact real-world business details throughout the profile. Do not create a listing for a location that customers cannot visit, and choose the service area that matches the business.

Suggested profile copy:

- **Business name:** Synergy IT
- **Primary category:** IT service provider
- **Website:** https://synergy-it.uk/
- **Service area:** United Kingdom; add specific towns or regions only where service is genuinely provided
- **Email:** info@synergy-it.uk
- **Short description:** Synergy IT helps ambitious businesses across the UK with managed IT support, cloud infrastructure, cyber security, digital workplace solutions and practical technology strategy.

Suggested services:

- Managed IT support
- IT support for UK businesses
- Cloud infrastructure services
- Cyber security for businesses
- Digital workplace solutions
- Technology strategy

Before publishing, add the logo, accurate opening hours, a phone number if available, and genuine business or team photos. Keep the name, website, email, service area and wording consistent with the website.

## Google Search Console

1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Add `https://synergy-it.uk/` as a Domain or URL-prefix property.
3. Verify ownership using the method Google provides.
4. Open **Sitemaps** and submit `https://synergy-it.uk/sitemap.xml`.
5. Use **URL inspection** to request indexing for the homepage, service pages and the three articles.
6. Check that the sitemap reports no fetch or parsing errors.

The sitemap currently contains these 10 public URLs:

- Homepage
- IT support UK
- Managed IT services
- Cyber security for businesses
- Cloud infrastructure services
- Technology strategy
- Digital workplace solutions
- What is managed IT support?
- Why cyber security matters
- Signs your business needs an IT review

## 90-day measurement plan

### Week 1

- Confirm the domain property and sitemap are verified.
- Record the initial indexed-page count.
- Confirm the contact form and `mailto:` link work.
- Link the Google Business Profile to the website.

### Weeks 2–4

- Check Search Console once per week.
- Record impressions, clicks, average position and top queries.
- Check indexing and page experience reports.
- Respond to genuine Business Profile reviews when they arrive.

### Month 2

- Identify queries with impressions but few clicks.
- Improve the relevant title, meta description or page introduction.
- Add internal links from relevant articles to service pages.
- Publish one useful article based on a real customer question.

### Month 3

- Compare clicks and impressions with the first month.
- Review which service pages attract search visibility.
- Improve pages ranking on positions 8–20 before creating more pages.
- Review Business Profile calls, website visits and direction requests if those metrics apply.

Track the same metrics each week rather than changing several pages at once. This makes it easier to connect an improvement with a measurable result.
